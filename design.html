The Look

The visual language of the calendar, in one place. Everything below is defined by name in styles/tokens.css; later parts of the app use those names rather than choosing colours or sizes of their own. Switch your computer between light and dark mode — this page follows it.

Colour

Warm paper rather than white, near-black ink, and a single accent used sparingly — today's date, the save button, an event's colour bar.

--color-paper The page itself
--color-surface Panels sitting on the page
--color-surface-sunken Recessed areas — days outside the month
--color-ink Primary text
--color-ink-muted Secondary text — weekday names, times
--color-line Decorative separators
--color-line-strong Outlines of things you interact with
--color-accent Today, primary action, event bars
--color-accent-soft A tint of the accent, for backgrounds
--color-danger Destructive actions only

Space

Every gap is a multiple of 4px, so the layout stays in rhythm. Shown to scale.

--space-1 through --space-8 · 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px

Buttons

One primary button is visible at a time — the single action that matters on screen. Everything else is quiet.

Text boxes

A label above, the box, and room underneath for an error. An invalid box is marked by its outline and by the message, never by colour alone.
